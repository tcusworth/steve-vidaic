04About

A Little About Me…

At my core, I'm a music producer, artist, composer, multi-instrumentalist, and creative collaborator. I've spent my career making records, touring and playing sessions, composing for film, working in post-production, and producing live and experiential projects.

Along the way, I've earned an Emmy for composition and Grammy recognition as a producer and engineer. I love making things, working with good people, and taking on projects that keep me interested and inspired.
